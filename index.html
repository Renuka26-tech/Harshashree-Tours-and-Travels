Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enquire about a tour?

Send your destination, travel dates, number of travellers and approximate budget through WhatsApp or the enquiry form. The team can then help with a suitable itinerary and quotation.

Can I customize a package?

Yes. You can request changes based on your dates, group size, budget, hotel preference, transport and special requirements.

Are the prices final?

No. Displayed prices are indicative starting prices. Final pricing depends on travel dates, availability, traveller count, hotel category, transport and selected inclusions.

Can you arrange local Hyderabad sightseeing?

Enquire about heritage and Old City tours, forts and palaces, temples, parks, food experiences, shopping, half-day and full-day sightseeing, and pickup/drop services.

Do you arrange cabs and transfers?

Local and outstation cabs, airport and railway transfers, one-way and round trips, and group vehicles can be enquired about, subject to route and vehicle availability.

What is your cancellation and payment policy?

Please ask the travel team for the applicable payment schedule, cancellation terms and booking conditions before confirming a trip.
